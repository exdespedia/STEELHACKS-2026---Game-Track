**THE LUCKIEST GUY - GDD**
Game Identity / Mantra: 
Following the sudden death of all the superheroes in the world, one college dropout takes on the role of stopping the (unfortunately not dead!) evildoers with nothing but some really, really crazy luck.

Jazzpunk-inspired first-person 3D action adventure game about a lucky superhero fighting bad guys.

**Design Pillars:**
Action
Comedy/corny
Meta but not to the point that its trying too hard
2010s time period (20xx)

**Genre/Story/Mechanics Summary:**
PROTAGONIST:
The main character is a lucky guy (who was not always lucky: there should be a funny reason why he is lucky). He is above 21 years old, and he gets all his income from winning the lottery (Unemployed).

INTRO: 
We see him gambling at a casino, where he wins it big – even the dealer is flabbergasted (the dealer is shown to be visibly cheating). He leaves the building with stacks of cash and comments on how his rent just got covered.
It’s odd how easily he got away with this…but that’s because he didn’t. A bunch of outraged gamblers and casino staff burst out the door behind him. —-> enter combat tutorial

ANTAGONIST: 
An unlucky superhero whose ability got every other superhero killed. Foil to the protag, he should be very passionate about his line of work.

SETTING: 
There used to be many superheroes, but they all died (There was a really strong bad guy who wiped them out. He should have a stupid ability like the mc’s luck: UNLUCKY?). The protag isn’t a hero, but uses his luck to play the role of hero in the others’ stead (huge ego)

STORY BEATS:
[OPENING/TUTORIAL] Mc gets a lot of money from the casino (5x jackpot winner), has to fight the casino owner (bad guy #1 and tutorial enemy). He suspects he may be lucky after this incident.
[LET THE PLAYER TEST WHAT THEY LEARNED IN 1] MC goes to spend his newfound money very responsibly on scratch tickets (to confirm if he’s actually lucky) and gets into some type of altercation, (maybe robbed?). Thats bad guy #2. He makes some witty comment about his win on the ticket then goes into beating the guy
MC officially starts his hero arc. Bad guy #3? #3 should be the most threatening bad guy, but the MC still wins because he is lucky. Should be a close call, hook into the main plot of the game.
The final boss (STRETCH GOAL AFTER STEELHACKS)
